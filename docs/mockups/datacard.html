Behaviour: Battler NPO

Moves towards the enemy to fight them, seeking cover on its way, and shoots if the need arises. When activated, it’s given an Engage order if it’s a NEMESIS NPO or will perform the Fight, Charge or Shoot action; otherwise a Conceal order.

Performs the first action it can from this list. If it can do none, it’s expended.

  1. Fight.
  2. Charge the closest player operative via the shortest possible route.
  3. Reposition towards the closest player operative, to cover if possible (a subsequent Dash can fulfil this).
  4. Shoot.
  5. Dash towards the closest player operative, to cover if possible.

Extra Defence: Whenever an operative is shooting this operative, collect and roll one additional defence dice, unless this operative is injured.

Bulky:

  • Cannot be placed on Vantage terrain higher than 2" from the killzone floor.
  • When it performs the Fight action, its control range can be 1" horizontally and 4" vertically until the end of that action.
  • Can move through other operatives (excluding NEMESIS). Ignore all changes to its APL.

Towering Size:

  • In the Firefight phase, it cannot be given a Conceal order.
  • Cannot be in cover or obscured by Light terrain, terrain parts less than 3" tall, or terrain that’s not wholly intervening.
  • Can be selected as a valid target while within control range of friendly operatives.
  • Moves through terrain parts less than 2" tall; cannot move through Accessible terrain (excl. hatchways) unless it’s also less than 2" tall. Terrain less than 2" tall where it finishes a move is temporarily removed.
